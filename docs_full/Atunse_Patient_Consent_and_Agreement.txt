ATUNSE HEALTHCARE LTD.
Patient Consent & Agreement
Informed consent for an investigational dual-route exosome regimen for dementia
You are being asked to take part in a research program at Atunse Healthcare Ltd. involving an investigational exosome treatment for dementia. This document explains the program, its risks and benefits, your rights, and how your information will be used. Please read it carefully (or have it read to you) and ask any questions before deciding. Your participation is voluntary and you may withdraw at any time without affecting your regular care.
1.  Purpose of this program
The purpose of this program is to evaluate, under medical supervision, an investigational regimen of two exosome products for the treatment of dementia, and to collect clinical and biomarker information. Atunse Healthcare Ltd. is the clinical site; KweHealth, LLC is the product sponsor.
Exosomes are tiny particles naturally produced by cells that carry signaling molecules between cells. The products are investigational — they are not approved medicines for dementia, and there is no claim that they cure, treat, prevent, or reverse the condition.
2.  What will happen if you take part
2.1  Treatment regimen
The standardized regimen is a course of six (6) treatment sessions given once weekly over six consecutive weeks. At each weekly session you will receive, on the same visit:
Intravenous: one 1 mL dose of Rejuvenate Serum™ (approximately 70 billion exosomes) by slow injection into a vein.
Intranasal: one 3 mL dose of ExoPure™ 140B (approximately 140 billion exosomes) delivered into the nose using the ViaNase™ device.
The dose is the same at every session; there is no dose increase. The supervising doctor confirms that each session is appropriate for you before it is given.
2.2  Clinical assessments
During the program your care team will collect your medical history, monitor you through examinations and cognitive/functional assessments, document any side effects, and conduct follow-up evaluations after the course.
2.3  Required biomarker testing
As part of the program you agree to undergo laboratory testing (including blood tests for inflammatory, metabolic, and other markers) before treatment (baseline), at intervals during and after the course, and additional tests if medically indicated. This testing is important for evaluating safety and response.
2.4  Time commitment and observation
You will attend six weekly treatment visits plus follow-up visits after the course. You will be observed in the clinic for approximately two hours after each session, and given instructions for home monitoring with your caregiver.
3.  Risks and discomforts
Common (may affect more than 1 in 10 people)
Nasal irritation or mild discomfort, mild headache, fatigue, temporary discomfort at the IV site.
Uncommon (may affect up to 1 in 10 people)
Allergic reactions, fever or flu-like symptoms, nausea, dizziness, nosebleed.
Rare but serious
Severe allergic reaction (anaphylaxis), infection, immune reactions, or other serious adverse events.
Because the products are investigational, there may be risks that are not yet known. You will be told of any important new risk information that arises. Blood draws may cause pain, bruising, or rarely infection or fainting. The effects on pregnancy and breastfeeding are unknown; tell your doctor if this may apply to you.
4.  Potential benefits
You may or may not benefit personally. Possible benefits include improvement in symptoms; however, benefit is not guaranteed, and your condition may remain unchanged or worsen. Information from your participation may help future patients and advance understanding of this treatment.
5.  Alternatives
Taking part is voluntary. Alternatives include standard dementia care and medications, supportive and non-drug approaches, management of other health conditions, and choosing no additional treatment. Your doctor can discuss these with you, including their risks and benefits.
6.  Costs and compensation
Participation in this program is undertaken on a private, fee-paying basis. The treatment sessions, biomarker testing, clinic visits, and associated care are billed to you in accordance with Atunse Healthcare Ltd.’s standard pricing schedule, which is provided to you separately and in writing before you consent. By taking part, you also contribute to the continued research and development of these investigational therapies for the benefit of future patients.
Please note the following so that your decision is fully informed: the treatment is investigational and payment does not guarantee any benefit or a particular outcome; your decision to participate is voluntary regardless of your ability to pay, and you may withdraw at any time as described in Section 8; and you will not be paid or otherwise compensated for taking part. If you are injured as a direct result of the treatment, you will receive appropriate medical care; the cost of that care may be billed to you or your insurer. Signing this form does not waive any legal rights you may have.
A detailed schedule of fees, including the cost per treatment session and the cost of biomarker testing and follow-up, is provided to you as a separate document. You are encouraged to review it carefully, and to ask any questions, before signing this consent.
7.  Confidentiality and use of your information
Information about your health, treatment, and test results will be collected and shared with the product sponsor (KweHealth, LLC) for research, safety monitoring, analysis, and possible scientific publication. Your privacy will be protected: you will be identified by a unique code rather than your name wherever possible, information will be stored securely with restricted access, and published results will not identify you.
Your information may be reviewed by your care team at Atunse Healthcare Ltd., the product sponsor and its authorized representatives, the testing laboratory, the responsible ethics committee, applicable regulatory authorities, and independent monitors or auditors. Information shared for research will be handled in accordance with applicable data-protection law. The specific data-protection law, ethics committee, and any regulatory authorizations that apply to this program at the site are recorded in the program file.
You may ask to see your records, ask for corrections, and withdraw your authorization at any time in writing. If you withdraw authorization, no new information will be collected, but information already collected may continue to be used for safety and research integrity.
8.  Voluntary participation and withdrawal
Your participation is voluntary. You may withdraw at any time without penalty and without affecting your regular care. If you withdraw, you may be asked to complete a final safety evaluation. Your doctor or the sponsor may also stop your participation if continuing could harm you, if you cannot complete required testing, or if the program is suspended for safety reasons. You will be told of any new information that might affect your willingness to continue.

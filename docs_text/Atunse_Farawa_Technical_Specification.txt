THE INTENDED OUTCOME
One Environment, Not Many Systems
Atunse Health today runs care across records, appointments, billing, communication and administration that do not speak to each other. This platform replaces that fragmentation with a single operating environment in which the entire patient lifecycle runs as one connected chain, with the Electronic Medical Record at its centre and the intelligence, trust and mobility layers built around it.
At completion of the initial programme, Atunse Health has a production-ready environment that supports the core patient lifecycle from registration through care, payment, follow-up and ongoing engagement, with existing patient data migrated in so that no patient starts again.
Patient → Registration → Consent → Scheduling → Clinical Care
Protocol → Billing → Follow-up → Governance → Intelligence
PLATFORM CAPABILITY ARCHITECTURE
Six Integrated Capability Domains
The platform comprises six capability domains. These are not separately commissioned products or separately priced modules. They are the architecture of one Atunse Health environment, and their value is that they operate together rather than in isolation.
1. EMR and Clinical Care
Outcome. Every patient has one authoritative clinical record, complete and current, accessible to authorised staff wherever care is delivered.
Patient registration, profiles and full medical history
Clinical encounters, consultation records and structured clinical notes
Vital signs, diagnoses and treatment records
Clinical forms, care pathways and referrals
Attachments, complete patient record history and clinical search
Role-based clinical access and a full clinical audit history
Existing patient data integration
Structured migration of agreed existing patient demographics and identifiers
Historical patient and clinical records brought into the new environment
Existing appointment and relevant historical administrative information
Migration governed by agreed source formats, data quality and mapping rules
2. Protocol and Clinical Intelligence Engine
Outcome. Atunse Health's clinical protocols stop being documents and institutional memory, and become active, executable components of daily operations that do not leave when staff do.
Clinical protocols, care pathways and decision rules
Protocol versioning, approval, deployment and exception handling
Workflow rules and protocol-linked forms
Protocol performance monitoring and deviation monitoring
Full protocol change history
AI-driven continuous learning
The platform learns from operational experience: Protocol, Execution, Data, AI Analysis, Human Review, Improvement
AI surfaces repeated exceptions, workflow bottlenecks, protocol deviations and emerging patterns
AI recommendations never override authorised clinical protocols; clinical authority remains with Atunse Health
3. CRM, Booking, Scheduling and Patient Engagement
Outcome. No enquiry is lost and no appointment goes untracked. Every patient is guided from first contact through booking, care and follow-up.
CRM
Patient and customer profiles, enquiries, leads and follow-ups
Communication and engagement history, relationship status and segmentation
Booking and scheduling
Appointment booking, provider availability and service calendars
Rescheduling, cancellations, reminders and follow-up scheduling
Ogami digital front office
Conversational enquiries, booking journeys and appointment information
Patient guidance, lead capture, guided and automated workflows
CRM-connected interactions with human escalation when needed
One connected journey: Enquiry, Booking, Care, Follow-up, Engagement
4. Billing, Payments and Revenue
Outcome. Every service rendered is captured, billed and reconciled, so revenue that currently leaks through untracked activity is closed off.
Service catalogue, charge creation and patient billing
Invoicing, payment recording and outstanding balances
Payment status, billing history and transaction records
Revenue reporting and administrative controls
A connected view: Patient, Service, Provider, Transaction, Revenue
5. People, Institutions and Administration
Outcome. The people, facilities and partner institutions that deliver care are mapped around the patient and the clinical record, not held in separate lists.
Personnel
Staff profiles, professional roles, departments, responsibilities and assignments
Availability, permissions, credential and status information, and activity records
Institutions and partners
Partner institutions, referring organisations, laboratories, pharmacies and specialists
Corporate partners, referral relationships and service relationships
Administration
Facilities, departments, users, roles and permissions
Operational settings, forms, notifications and workflow administration
6. Trust, Consent, Chain of Custody and Governance
Outcome. Consent and critical events are verifiable and tamper-evident, so Atunse Health can establish what happened, when, and under whose authority, with confidence.
Consent management
Consent capture, type, status, version, timestamp, giver and purpose
Consent modification, withdrawal, full history and verification of consent state
Consent becomes an active digital state rather than a static document
Chain of custody
Tamper-evident tracking of custody transfer, receipt and handover
Specimen or sample movement, critical records, documents, approvals and authorisations
Equipment and other controlled assets, and significant clinical or administrative events
Establishes: Who, Did What, When, Under What Authority, To What, What Happened Next
Administrative and governance tracking
Administrative and protocol approvals, authorisations and critical access events
Record status changes, governance decisions and administrative signatures
A blockchain-backed ledger provides a tamper-evident verification layer for these events
Sensitive patient records remain in the secure data layer, not on a public blockchain
ACCESS ANYWHERE
Mobile and Offline Capability
Outcome. The system is usable in real healthcare environments, on the devices staff already have, and does not stop working when the network does.
The platform is delivered as a Progressive Web Application, so authorised users reach it on any supported phone, tablet or desktop without an app store deployment.
Mobile
Responsive clinical interface and mobile patient registration
Mobile clinical workflows, scheduling and administrative functions
Mobile access to authorised patient information and notifications
Offline
Offline data capture and local workflow continuation
Temporary local storage with synchronisation when connectivity returns
Conflict handling and clear synchronisation status
Applied selectively to appropriate workflows, because not every clinical operation should permit unrestricted offline access
BUILT TO GROW
Multi-Tenant Platform Architecture
Outcome. Atunse Health can expand beyond the initial deployment without replacing the underlying technology.
The platform is built on a multi-tenant architecture that supports multiple organisational environments while maintaining appropriate separation of data and access. This creates the foundation for future expansion across additional facilities, departments, services, operational units, locations and partner organisations.

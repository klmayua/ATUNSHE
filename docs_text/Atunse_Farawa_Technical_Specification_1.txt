SECTION 1
The Intended Outcome
Atunse Health today runs care across records, appointments, billing, communication and administration that do not speak to each other. This platform replaces that fragmentation with a single operating environment in which the entire patient lifecycle runs as one connected chain, with a patient-centred Electronic Medical Record at its centre and the intelligence, trust and mobility layers built around it.
At completion of the initial programme, Atunse Health has a production-ready environment supporting the core patient lifecycle from registration through care, payment, follow-up and ongoing engagement, with agreed existing patient data migrated in so that no patient starts again.

Governance and intelligence run across every stage, so that the record stays accurate, trusted and continuously improving.
SECTION 2
Platform Architecture
The platform is organised as layered capability, not a set of disconnected tools. Patients and staff reach it through an access layer; clinical and operational work happens in the application domains; a trust layer secures consent, custody and governance; an intelligence layer learns from operations; and a multi-tenant foundation carries it all.

SECTION 3
EMR and Clinical Care
Outcome.  Every patient has one authoritative, longitudinal record that they help build and can access, and that authorised staff can rely on wherever care is delivered.
The record is patient-centred by design. It is organised around the patient rather than around a department or a visit, so a patient's whole history sits in one timeline, one identity carries across every facility, and the patient participates in their own record rather than being a passive subject of it. Practitioner tools exist to serve that record accurately and quickly.

A single longitudinal record per patient: one timeline of every encounter, result, prescription and interaction
One patient identity that carries across all facilities, departments and visits

Patient access and participation (patient-driven)
Patient portal access to their own record, results and care plans where authorised
Patient-entered demographics, history and pre-visit questionnaires that flow straight into the record
Patient-reported symptoms and outcomes captured before, during and after care
Patient view of upcoming and past appointments, reminders and instructions
Patient-held consent and communication preferences, editable by the patient

Configurable clinical forms and templates, with free-text and structured capture

SECTION 9
Mobile and Offline Capability
Outcome.  The system is usable in real healthcare environments, on the devices staff and patients already have, and does not stop working when the network does.
The platform is delivered as a Progressive Web Application, so authorised users reach it on any supported phone, tablet or desktop without an app-store deployment, and selected workflows continue working through unreliable connectivity.
Mobile
Responsive clinical and patient interfaces on any device
Mobile patient registration, clinical workflows and scheduling
Mobile access to authorised patient information and notifications
Offline
Offline data capture and local workflow continuation
Temporary encrypted local storage with synchronisation when connectivity returns
Conflict handling and clear synchronisation status
Applied selectively to appropriate workflows, because not every clinical operation should permit unrestricted offline access
